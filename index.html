What killed Ghana's savings & loans companies

All 23 savings and loans companies and finance houses whose licences the Bank of Ghana revoked on 16 August 2019. Every figure is transcribed by hand from Annex 2 of the revocation notice.

Source: Bank of Ghana, Notice of Revocation of Licences of Insolvent Savings and Loans Companies and Finance Houses, 16 August 2019. n = 23.

Analysis and dataset: savings_loans_verified.csv. Causes are as stated by the regulator and hand-coded from the notice; they reflect what the Bank of Ghana chose to publish. With n = 23 these are descriptive findings, not statistical ones.
