Explore the data

The 23 savings and loans companies and finance houses whose licences the Bank of Ghana revoked on 16 August 2019. Every number on this page is a column of a SQL view in this repository. The page sorts and filters, but calculates nothing.

Source: Bank of Ghana, Notice of Revocation of Licences of Insolvent Savings and Loans Companies and Finance Houses, 16 August 2019, Annex 2. n = 23.

Headline figures

Which causes the notice cites together

Read across a row. Each cell counts the institutions citing the row’s cause that also cite the column’s. A pair only means something against the institutions that do not cite the row’s cause, so select a cell to see that comparison.

One institution at a time

Everything the notice gives for each of the 23, with all ten causes. “Not cited” means the notice does not state that cause for the institution. It does not mean the cause was absent.

Causes by licence type

The share of each licence type whose notice cites the cause. There are 16 savings and loans companies and only 7 finance houses, so a single finance house moves its bar by 14 points. Read the counts, not the lengths. The notice gives no location, so licence type is the only breakdown the data supports.

Time licensed while insolvent, by cause Exploratory

Choose a cause to split the 20 institutions with a stated insolvency date into those whose notice cites it and those whose notice does not. Each dot is one institution. The dark tick marks each group’s median. One side of the split often holds only a handful of institutions, so a gap between medians describes these institutions and nothing wider.

View all ten causes as a table

Where the numbers come from. scripts/build_dashboard_data.py loads the published CSVs, runs the views in sql/bi/, refuses to continue unless every reconciliation check passes, and writes the data this page reads. The model is described in docs/bi-data-model.md, and each headline figure is defined, with its query and caveats, in docs/kpi-definitions.md.

Causes are as stated by the regulator and hand-coded from the notice; they reflect what the Bank of Ghana chose to publish. With n = 23 these are descriptive findings, not statistical ones.

Notice. This page reports what the Bank of Ghana’s published notices state. It does not independently allege wrongdoing by any institution or individual. The failure causes are the Bank’s assessment, not the author’s. The institution view covers all 23, including GN Savings and Loans, whose revocation is before the courts; its entry carries a note on the litigation, and this project takes no position on it. Corrections are welcome and will be made.
